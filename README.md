## About

I design and ship end-to-end software systems with a strong emphasis on reliable engineering, clean architecture, and measurable product impact. My work sits at the intersection of **AI** engineering, full-stack development, and pragmatic product thinking, with a bias toward building features that move real metrics.

I am comfortable going from problem definition to production deployment: clarifying requirements, designing APIs, implementing services, and instrumenting systems with the right telemetry. On the AI/ML side, I focus on practical applications of NLP and LLMs, from embedding-based retrieval to model-powered workflows, using Python and modern ML tooling.

Beyond shipping code, I care deeply about developer experience, maintainability, and resilience—designing systems that are observable, secure, and built for iteration. I enjoy collaborating with product teams, translating ambiguous ideas into concrete technical plans, and aligning engineering decisions with business outcomes.

### Open To

- AI Engineer / ML Engineer roles with strong product collaboration  
- Full-stack or backend engineering roles owning services end-to-end  
- Product-focused engineering roles (0→1 and early-stage platforms)  
- Research-driven prototyping in applied NLP / LLMs  
- Open source contributions in AI tooling, agents, and infrastructure
